## Description (≤ 10,000 characters)
```
Close the lid, your AI keeps working.

You start an AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor…), a long build, a download or a render, then want to close the laptop or walk away? Windows goes to sleep and everything stops. KeepLid keeps the PC awake exactly as long as needed, then hands control back automatically.

ONE CLICK
• Click the icon next to the clock: on / off.
• The icon shows the state; the tooltip shows the time left ("Awake — lid OK — 1 h 42 left").
• Optional global shortcut (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A by default).

TURNS ITSELF OFF
• Timer: 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h or a custom duration.
• Until a process ends: KeepLid stays on while the chosen process runs (claude, node, python, codex, cursor, ollama, code…), then turns off and tells you: "Task finished. The PC can go back to sleep."

LID CLOSED
• Lid mode: KeepLid temporarily sets "When I close the lid" to "Do nothing", then puts your original setting back.
• By default on mains power only; on battery only after your explicit confirmation.
• On some PCs, Windows only lets administrators change this setting. KeepLid never asks for administrator rights: it detects this and walks you through changing it yourself in 30 seconds.

NOTHING LEFT CHANGED
• The original setting is saved before any change.
• Automatic restore when you turn it off, quit, sign out, shut down, when the PC goes to sleep, and even after a crash (at the next launch).
• "Restore original power settings now" button in the settings.

SAFETY LIMITS
• Low battery (20% by default): automatic turn-off and notification.
• Maximum time with the lid closed on battery (2 hours by default).
• KeepLid never simulates keystrokes or mouse moves: it uses the official Windows mechanism to signal that work is in progress.

PRIVACY
• 100% local: no internet connection, no telemetry, no account.
• No personal data collected. A small local log (1 MB max) stays on your PC for support.

GOOD TO KNOW
• With the lid closed, the built-in screen turns off: that is hardware. The PC itself keeps working.
• The lid setting is changed temporarily, then restored automatically.
• Never leave a running laptop in a closed bag or on a soft surface: it needs air. KeepLid does not read temperatures (that would require administrator rights); its safety limits are based on battery level and time.
• Product names mentioned (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor…) belong to their respective owners. KeepLid is an independent app, not affiliated with these publishers.

Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, x64 and ARM64. English and French.
```
