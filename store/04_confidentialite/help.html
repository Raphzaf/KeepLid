KeepLid help

"Close the lid, your AI keeps working." · Français · Privacy policy

1. Why does the screen turn off when I close the lid while KeepLid is on?

That is normal and hardware: closing the lid turns off the built-in screen (lid sensor / hinge). KeepLid prevents sleep, not the internal screen from turning off. The PC keeps working: your AI agent, build or download keeps going. Open the lid to see the result.

2. The menu shows "Lid closed: setup guide…" instead of a check box. Why?

On some PCs (often company-managed ones, or depending on how Windows is set up), Windows only lets administrators change the power plan. KeepLid never asks for administrator rights; it detects this at startup and offers a guide to set "When I close the lid" to "Do nothing" yourself (30 seconds), then reminds you to set it back at the end. Sleep prevention works normally.

3. Does it drain my battery?

An awake PC uses more power than a sleeping one, especially with the lid closed and a heavy task. That is why the lid mode only acts on mains power by default; on battery it asks for your confirmation; and KeepLid turns itself off below 20% battery and after 2 hours with the lid closed on battery (both adjustable).

4. Is it dangerous for my laptop?

A running laptop must be able to get rid of its heat. Never leave it running in a closed bag or on a soft surface (bed, sofa). KeepLid does not read temperatures (that would require administrator rights): its safety limits are based on battery and time. Windows and the hardware keep their own thermal protections.

5. Does KeepLid change my Windows settings?

Only the "When I close the lid" setting of the active power plan, and only when you turn on the lid mode. The original value is saved before the change and put back automatically: when you turn it off, quit, sign out, shut down, when the PC sleeps and, after a crash, at the next launch. If you change that setting yourself meanwhile, KeepLid keeps your new choice.

6. My PC crashed (or I killed KeepLid) during lid mode. Is my setting lost?

No. A small watchdog process restores the setting if KeepLid is killed. If everything stopped at once (power cut), the restore happens at the next launch of KeepLid (turn on "Start with Windows" to make it automatic). You can also click Settings › "Restore original power settings now". Manual check: powercfg /q SCHEME_CURRENT SUB_BUTTONS LIDACTION.

7. Does KeepLid send data? Do I need an account?

No. KeepLid is 100% local: no internet connection, no telemetry, no account. See the privacy policy.

8. How do I keep the PC awake until my AI agent finishes?

Right-click the icon › Keep awake › "Until a process ends…", check the process (for example claude, node, python, codex, cursor, ollama, code) then Start. KeepLid turns off about 15 seconds after the last checked process exits and shows "Task finished".

9. Does KeepLid simulate mouse moves?

No. It uses the official Windows API (SetThreadExecutionState) that tells the system work is in progress. No keystroke or mouse move is simulated, and your screen lock still works unless you turn on "Keep the screen on".

10. How do I uninstall KeepLid cleanly?

Quit KeepLid (right-click › "Quit (restores everything)"): every setting is put back. Then Windows Settings › Apps › KeepLid › Uninstall. Its files (preferences, log) are removed with the app; nothing is left in the registry.
